# Transport Network Seismic Resilience — Master's Thesis

**Official thesis title:** Analysis of Indicators to Assess the Resilience of Transport Networks in an Interregional Context

**Author:** Seyedreza Aghayarikordkandi  
**University:** University of Naples Federico II, Italy  
**Degree:** MSc in Transportation Engineering and Mobility  
**Academic Year:** 2024/2025

## Overview

This research evaluates the resilience of interregional road networks in Southern Italy under seismic disruption scenarios.

The study integrates graph-theoretical network indicators, GIS-based spatial analysis, and transportation modelling to investigate changes in connectivity, accessibility, and network redundancy following earthquake-induced infrastructure disruptions.

## Research Methods

- Graph-theoretical analysis of transport network connectivity
- Betweenness centrality and node criticality assessment
- Beta Index, Gamma Index, clustering coefficient, and average degree
- GIS-based spatial analysis of seismic disruption scenarios
- PTV Visum transport network modelling
- Network redundancy and travel-time impact assessment

## Study Area

The research focuses on the interregional transportation network of Southern Italy, with particular attention to Campania and its connections with neighbouring regions.

## Key Findings

The study identifies the A16 motorway as a critical corridor for east–west connectivity, particularly between Campania and Puglia.

High-intensity seismic disruption scenarios reveal substantial network fragmentation, reduced accessibility, and limited alternative routes in vulnerable areas.

## Repository Contents

This repository contains the LaTeX source files, bibliography, figures, and supporting thesis materials included in the shared project archive.

## Research Scope

The analysis is based on modelled seismic disruption scenarios and network resilience indicators. It is not an empirical prediction of damage from a specific future earthquake.
